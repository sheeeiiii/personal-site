Project - Rave Dice

    An interactive dice game. Players roll two physical dice and try to hit the 50 point target. It triggers sounds and visual effects.

  • Physical dice powered by micro:bit.
  • A webpage that displays the dice results.
  • Sounds and colours inspired by a rave.
